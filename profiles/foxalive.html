Terra Firma · Ignis Dimension

FOXALIVE

The Monster Killer.

Born September 29, 1979, in the Kirai Dimension, FOXALIVE was brought to the Ignis Dimension as a very young child with his separated parents. Telepathy and telekinesis were part of his life from birth. In a world that insisted such powers did not exist, revealing them could be deadly.

The Survivor

His childhood was marked by abuse, abandonment, and institutions that offered little refuge. His mother struggled under pressure and sometimes lashed out violently. At nine, he went to live with his father, whose cruelty, along with that of his new family, grew far worse. At fourteen, FOXALIVE was placed in a children's home. By eighteen, the system had released him into homelessness, with little more than the clothes on his back.

Seeking nothing more than a shower and a moment of compassion, he returned to his father's home. Instead, his father and half brother resumed their psychological torment. FOXALIVE finally reached his breaking point and stopped their hearts with telekinesis. Their deaths were judged natural; investigators never found him.

The First Monster

Not long afterward, a creature called a Lachesis attacked him. The fight dragged on as FOXALIVE struggled with emerging magic he did not yet understand. He tackled the monster, forced his fist down its throat, and discovered that the explosive magic gathering in his hand could be triggered with a snap of his fingers. The creature was destroyed. The alley was nearly torn apart.

Contact with the Lachesis left him gravely ill. He stumbled into a familiar park, where trees, plants, and wildlife concealed him while the Power of Nature helped give his body time to recover. When he finally awakened, ravenously hungry, he knew something the rest of the world preferred to deny: monsters were real.

And if monsters were real, there were people who would benefit from their deaths.

The Monster Killer

FOXALIVE began deliberately tracking and hunting monsters. His telepathy and telekinesis, practiced since childhood, gave him advantages that only grew with experience. He refined his defensive telekinesis, developed his magic, acquired a base of operations, and eventually took contracts to eliminate monstrous threats for corporations willing to pay for the work.

He also used his extraordinary abilities to find financial opportunities, building resources and independence along the way. He built his estate brick by brick, without knowing the Celestial bloodline sleeping within him—or the larger history of the Kava bloodline.

Across other realms, where time does not always pass as it does on Terra Firma, he learned, fought, loved, lost, and found people who became family. His abilities expanded, including the Shinigami Eyes: normally ice-blue eyes that turn azure, their pupils becoming slender four-pointed black stars when activated.

Back in Ignis, his life continued beyond the hunt. His path crossed the World League Wrestling Federation, April Nanakusa, Yuka Hayabusa, Kanako Uribo, and the women who would become the Razorwire Chainsaw Dolls.

His mother eventually apologized for her part in his childhood, and he forgave her. She later died in a hospital after failures in her care—another loss in a life that had never been short of them.

FOXALIVE has never claimed to be a paragon of virtue. He's the Monster Killer. What he does best isn't pretty. But he's the best.

In Ignis, the names and titles of distant Celestial history mean little. There's just FOXALIVE. And they don't know what to do with just one of him.
