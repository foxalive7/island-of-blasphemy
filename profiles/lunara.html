Island Staff · Elf · Newshire, Ohio

Lunara “Luni” DeContere

Accounting

Born December 3, 1996, in Newshire, an elven village hidden among rural Ohio cornfields, Luni is a mathematical genius with an extraordinary gift for accounting. Her abstract thinking and unconventional approach to problems make her invaluable to the Island.

Her parents, Rumi and Hensley DeContere, raised her in a loving, traditional elven household. Hensley farms corn and dairy, using magic to conduct trade with humans. Luni’s fascination with human culture sometimes puts her at odds with Newshire’s distrustful residents.

Shy and reluctant to confront others, Luni nevertheless has a genuinely golden heart. She is an attentive, loyal friend whose warmth becomes apparent once she feels comfortable.

A Few Favorites

Mathematics, music, JRPGs—including Chrono Trigger, Chrono Cross, Dragon Quest and Final Fantasy—Neverwinter Nights, Star Wars: The Old Republic, fighting games, strategy games, and creative problem-solving.
