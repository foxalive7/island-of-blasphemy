The Women of Terra Firma

April Nanakusa

The Artist Behind the Art

Graphic Artist • Illustrator • VTuber & VRChat Model Creator

Born on August 21, 1995, April Nanakusa grew up in Camp Washington, Cincinnati, Ohio, with her single mother.

Her mother had embraced gothic fashion and culture long before it became fashionable, and April naturally followed in her footsteps. But the greatest gift her mother gave her wasn't an appreciation for black clothing or unconventional aesthetics.

It was a love of art.

From her earliest childhood doodles, April displayed an extraordinary talent for drawing. Her mother recognized that gift and encouraged her to explore it, providing the love and support that helped a childhood interest develop into a lifelong passion.

Unfortunately, their home life was not always peaceful.

April's mother struggled with drug addiction and a succession of unhealthy relationships. Although she remained a loving and gentle presence in her daughter's life, her difficulties frequently placed April in the position of providing emotional—and sometimes physical—support.

Despite these challenges, April excelled academically.

Art came naturally to her, but she worked especially hard in her other subjects, determined to build an education worthy of her ambitions.

Her efforts eventually earned her a scholarship to the University of Cincinnati.

It should have been one of the happiest periods of her young life.

Instead, tragedy struck.

Returning home one afternoon, April discovered that her mother had died.

Whether her death resulted from foul play or the consequences of her long struggle with addiction remained uncertain.

What April knew was that the most important person in her life was gone.

For all the difficulties they had endured, mother and daughter had been constants in each other's lives. They had loved one another, supported one another, and faced their circumstances together.

Now April was alone.

Following a period of mourning, she began pouring her grief into her artwork. The creative passion her mother had nurtured became both an emotional outlet and a means of moving forward.

It was during her college years that April met FOXALIVE.

Recognizing that she needed support, he refused to let her attempts to push him away become the end of their friendship. He continued showing up, talking with her, and offering a steady presence without demanding that she become someone she wasn't.

Over time, April discovered just how much his friendship meant to her.

Through their conversations and shared experiences, her personality continued to develop into the wonderfully contradictory combination she is today.

A goth gremlin with a sharp tongue, considerable street smarts, fierce independence, and a wisdom that often seems beyond her years.

April eventually graduated summa cum laude with a degree in graphic art.

Following graduation, she moved into FOXALIVE's estate, becoming his roommate and acquiring a front-row seat to the extraordinary assortment of adventures and shenanigans that seemed to follow him everywhere.

Her professional career flourished.

Working as a freelance artist, April developed a formidable reputation for distinctive, imaginative artwork and uncompromising professionalism. She established clear expectations regarding payment, took her commitments seriously, and made certain that clients received the work they had commissioned.

Her abilities and reputation eventually allowed her to expand into creating models for VTubers and VRChat, adding new dimensions to her already considerable artistic portfolio.

When Razorwire Chainsaw Dolls became part of the extended circle surrounding FOXALIVE, April welcomed the women into her life.

She soon became an important part of the band's creative identity, producing artwork for their music and albums while maintaining her independent freelance career.

Online, April is respected not only for her artistic abilities but also for her refusal to participate in unnecessary drama.

She advertises her work, communicates with clients, fulfills her obligations, and generally avoids becoming consumed by internet culture.

After all, she has a life outside the screen.

With Kanako, Miki, Saya, Yuka, Shizuka, and FOXALIVE surrounding her, April has developed a close-knit circle of friends who provide more than enough companionship, laughter, and opportunities for mischief.

She's still independent.

Still snarky.

Still perfectly capable of telling someone exactly what she thinks.

But the young woman who once faced an uncertain future after losing her mother has built something remarkable.

A successful career. A home. Friendships. A life filled with people who genuinely care about her.

And through it all, April continues doing the very thing her mother encouraged her to do so many years ago.

She makes art.
