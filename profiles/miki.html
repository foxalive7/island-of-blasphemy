The Women of Terra Firma

Miki Shima

Miki Danger — Rhythm Guitar & Backing Vocals

Razorwire Chainsaw Dolls

Born on February 20, 1998, Miki Shima grew up in the affluent Cincinnati suburb of Blue Ash, Ohio. The daughter of a well-paid corporate employee and a restaurant hostess who worked largely for the enjoyment of it, Miki enjoyed a comfortable upbringing with parents who supported her interests—even when those interests involved some of the loudest and most aggressive music imaginable.

From an early age, Miki developed a passion for heavy metal. Venom, Motörhead, Megadeth, Slayer, and Black Sabbath were among her favorites, while Alice Cooper's theatrical performances particularly captured her imagination.

Her parents were amused by their daughter's musical tastes, but they took her ambitions seriously. They paid for guitar lessons, and on Christmas Day in 2011, Miki received her first guitar.

She never looked back.

By 2018, Miki had already performed with bands including Danger and Elfen Blood, gaining valuable experience and refining her abilities as a guitarist and performer.

There was, however, one persistent complication in her life.

Miki stood just 4 feet, 9 inches tall, having stopped growing in height around 2016. Despite her unmistakably adult figure, her petite stature and youthful appearance frequently led strangers to mistake her for a child.

Even her twenty-first birthday became an exercise in absurdity when she needed someone else to purchase alcohol for her own celebration. At bars, clubs, and music venues, she routinely encountered questions about whether her parents knew where she was.

Miki learned to take much of it in stride, but she has little patience for anyone who makes her height into a spectacle.

Fortunately, Miki has never lacked personality.

Exuberant, boisterous, fiercely independent, and unapologetically feminine, she happily embraces her own cuteness while displaying the mischievous energy of a self-proclaimed gremlin. She loves a good party, enjoys meeting people, and rarely has difficulty making friends.

In 2019, Miki met fellow musicians Yuka Hayabusa, Saya Hayashima, and Shizuka Hagimoto at a club. Their shared love of music quickly developed into friendship, strengthened through countless evenings of drinking, smoking, and spending time together.

The group's eventual meeting with Kanako Uribo completed what would become Razorwire Chainsaw Dolls.

Kanako quickly assumed the role of the group's beloved big sister: someone the other women could rely upon when they needed advice, reassurance, or simply a steady presence.

For Miki, the band's formation represented more than another opportunity to perform.

Although her life had been filled with opportunities and largely positive experiences, the relationships she developed with Kanako, Saya, Yuka, Shizuka, April Nanakusa, and FOXALIVE brought her a depth of happiness and belonging she had never previously experienced.

Today, performing under the stage name Miki Danger, she provides rhythm guitar and backing vocals for Razorwire Chainsaw Dolls.

She's still loud. Still mischievous. Still the life of the party.

And at four feet, nine inches tall, she's living proof that a musician doesn't need to be physically imposing to make an enormous amount of noise.

Just don't make her height weird.
