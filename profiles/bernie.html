Bernadette “Bernie” Mitchellson

Customer Service

Bernadette Mitchellson can sell you on an idea, outlift you at the gym, and make you laugh hard enough to forget what you were complaining about—all before lunch. Short, strong, gloriously outspoken, and armed with an absolutely devastating grin, Bernie is a force of nature with a surprisingly gentle heart.

She’s worked as a bouncer, bartender, and high-performing call-center salesperson, picking up an impressive talent for dealing with people along the way. Her professional customer-service voice is sweet enough to fool almost anyone. Her actual personality? Considerably louder, much funnier, and refreshingly impossible to mistake for anyone else’s.

Bernie loves competition, gaming, working out, and Cincinnati football. She also loves her family fiercely—especially her cousin Estelle, who’s been her best friend since childhood. Behind the jokes and bravado is someone who shows up when it counts.

A Few Favorites

Lifting weights, video games, Bengals games, friendly competition, a good night out, and making Estelle laugh.
