HER STORY

Alruna Nilou is a 114-year-old Arisa sorceress in the prime of her life. A veteran monster hunter, she commands high-grade telekinesis and powerful sorcery, with luck among her gifts. She has faced and killed numerous monsters, earning respect through experience rather than reputation alone.

Confident, ruthlessly intelligent and compassionate toward those close to her, Alruna balances tomboyish directness with elegance and poise. She knows what she wants and prefers games she expects to win—but also knows when to relax and enjoy the moment.

Away from danger, she enjoys hot springs, good books, fantasy and horror, JRPGs and strategy games. She's no morning person, and takes her coffee with plenty of sweetener, milk, and either chocolate or caramel.
