The Women of Terra Firma

Shizuka Hagimoto

Shizuka Chainsaw — Drums

Razorwire Chainsaw Dolls

Born on December 2, 1995, in Cincinnati's affluent Western Hills area, Shizuka Hagimoto has spent practically her entire life doing things her own way.

Long before she knew there was a name for it, Shizuka was already living the gyaru lifestyle.

As a child, she spent countless hours running, playing, and enjoying the sunshine. Her skin developed a natural bronze tan that deepened during Cincinnati's warmer months. She was energetic, outgoing, and possessed a seemingly inexhaustible appetite for fun.

Then she met Saya Hayashima.

The two girls quickly became inseparable, sharing afternoons at the park, imaginary adventures, music, and whatever food they could get their hands on.

For Shizuka, food was one of life's great pleasures.

For Saya, it was sometimes something she had to sacrifice.

Growing up in poverty, Saya frequently ate smaller portions to ensure her two brothers and sister had enough. Shizuka recognized what was happening and quietly made certain her friend had something to eat during the school day.

She didn't make a spectacle of her generosity.

Saya was her friend. That was reason enough.

As the girls grew older, their public personalities developed in very different directions.

Shizuka became the undisputed Queen Bee of their school.

Students sought her opinions, offered her food, and looked to her for approval. Even teachers found themselves accommodating her considerable social influence. Shizuka understood exactly how much sway she possessed and, more importantly, knew when to exercise it.

Saya, meanwhile, was usually right beside her.

Their friendship remained rooted in those childhood afternoons when neither girl needed anything more than imagination, music, and each other's company.

When Shizuka eventually discovered that her preferred fashion and lifestyle had a name—gyaru—she embraced it wholeheartedly.

But Shizuka never needed a trend to tell her how to live.

She did what she wanted because she wanted to do it.

During high school, Shizuka participated in gymnastics, softball, and soccer alongside Saya. She also discovered the drums, often practicing during the late afternoons after school.

Drumming offered something she hadn't found anywhere else: a uniquely satisfying outlet for her energy.

Unlike some of her future bandmates, Shizuka didn't build her musical identity around a particular collection of favorite bands. She enjoyed music broadly, although Sepultura held a special place in her tastes because of its drumming.

And beneath her apparently carefree personality, Shizuka possessed a surprisingly demanding streak.

Whether practicing gymnastics, competing in sports, or learning a difficult drum passage, she could become intensely focused on the smallest details.

She wanted things right.

Not necessarily because anyone expected perfection from her, but because she expected it from herself.

By the time Shizuka and Saya graduated, their school had grown so accustomed to Shizuka's presence and influence that her departure seemed to leave a genuine void.

Adulthood did little to diminish her personality.

With bronzed skin, blonde hair tipped in platinum, a curvaceous figure, and a wicked smile, Shizuka is an unmistakably glamorous woman who knows exactly how attractive she is.

She's playful, flirtatious, occasionally crude, and entirely comfortable with attention. If someone steals another glance at her figure, she's unlikely to lose sleep over it.

That's their problem, not hers.

She's also the kind of woman who might ask whether you're buying her dinner, then cheerfully hand you the menu.

Her longtime friendship with Saya eventually led her into the circle of musicians who would become Razorwire Chainsaw Dolls.

Performing as Shizuka Chainsaw, she provides the thunderous percussion behind the band's music, bringing the same meticulous attention to her drumming that she once devoted to sports and school activities.

Today, Shizuka enjoys a life that allows her to indulge her passions, celebrate her friendships, and express herself without restraint.

She doesn't need permission to be glamorous.

She doesn't need approval to be confident.

And she certainly doesn't need anyone telling her to turn down the volume.

Shizuka Hagimoto has always lived loud.

Now she gets to make a career out of it.
