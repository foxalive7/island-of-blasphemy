The Women of Terra Firma

Saya Hayashima

Saya “Hydrogen” — Lead Guitar

Razorwire Chainsaw Dolls

Born on May 26, 1996, Saya Hayashima has always believed that elegance is something a woman creates for herself, not something determined by the balance of her bank account.

Growing up in Cincinnati, Ohio, Saya knew poverty firsthand. Her parents were loving and devoted, but both worked at least two jobs to support their family. Money was frequently scarce, and the household's financial circumstances offered few opportunities for extravagance.

Saya refused to let those circumstances define her.

From an early age, she developed a fascination with glamour, theatrical presentation, and the extraordinary spectacle of professional wrestling. Japanese women's wrestling, commonly known as joshi, became a particular passion. She admired the elaborate costumes, commanding entrances, and larger-than-life personalities of its performers.

Rather than simply imitate her favorites, Saya began developing an aesthetic entirely her own.

Her elaborate, bird-inspired outfits are creations of her own hands, combining elegance, drama, and an unmistakable edge. Her glamorous makeup draws inspiration from one of her favorite wrestlers, incorporating personal adjustments that have become part of her signature appearance.

With long, dark hair, smoky eyes, and a strikingly feminine figure, Saya possesses the kind of beauty that can appear inviting one moment and positively dangerous the next.

Her musical interests are equally distinctive.

Drawing inspiration from King Diamond, Iron Maiden, Jethro Tull, Fleetwood Mac, Pink Floyd, and Rush, Saya developed an eclectic appreciation for musicianship, theatricality, atmosphere, and musical experimentation.

Like her friend and fellow musician Yuka Hayabusa, Saya taught herself to play guitar. Without being confined to a single formal approach, she developed her own style, allowing her diverse influences to shape her playing.

Throughout her childhood and adolescence, one of the most important constants in Saya's life was Shizuka Hagimoto.

The two grew up together, attended the same Cincinnati public high school, and participated in sports including gymnastics, softball, and soccer. Their friendship endured through the changes of adolescence and into adulthood, eventually becoming one of the closest relationships within Razorwire Chainsaw Dolls.

When Saya joined Kanako Uribo, Miki Shima, Yuka Hayabusa, and Shizuka Hagimoto in Razorwire Chainsaw Dolls, she brought more than her considerable guitar-playing ability.

She brought spectacle.

Performing as Saya “Hydrogen”, she approaches the stage with the confidence and theatrical instincts of a professional wrestler making a championship entrance.

Her elaborate entrance attire, striking makeup, and commanding presence make her impossible to overlook. At times, she carries her guitar like a standard into battle, accompanied by a flag proudly bearing the words:

SCISSOR QUEEN

For Saya, performance is an opportunity to transform music into something larger than sound alone.

Every outfit, every entrance, every note is an expression of a woman who learned long ago that circumstances do not determine personal worth.

She came from poverty.

She created her own glamour.

And when Saya Hydrogen takes the stage, she makes absolutely certain the audience knows she's arrived.
