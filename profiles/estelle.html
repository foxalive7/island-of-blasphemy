Estelle Arthur

Hostess

Estelle Arthur has a way of making people feel welcome before they’ve even found their seats. Warm, poised, quick-witted, and just mischievous enough to keep things interesting, she’s the kind of hostess who can handle a difficult evening without ever losing her smile—or her sense of humor.

A Cincinnati native and University of Cincinnati graduate, Estelle brings years of hospitality experience to the Island of Blasphemy. She loves good conversation, good coffee, classic games, and her beloved Bengals. Off the clock, she’s much more likely to be found in a comfortable hoodie, cheering at the television with her cousin Bernie, than dressed for a formal evening.

Estelle is a romantic at heart, with an enormous capacity for affection and a talent for finding joy in the little things. She’ll happily make a holiday celebration bigger, brighter, and more ridiculous if it means sharing that happiness with the people she loves.

A Few Favorites

Cincinnati Bengals, Final Fantasy VII and VIII, Chrono Trigger, Zone of the Enders, parks, flowers, birds, coffee, pizza, and spectacularly excessive Christmas decorations.
