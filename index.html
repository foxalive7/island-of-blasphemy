ABOUT THE CREATOR

Foxalive — Writer, content creator, streamer, musician, and worldbuilder.

I want my work to give people a place where they can enjoy themselves and forget about the real world for a while. Characters that make you laugh, cry, feel things for—the whole gauntlet.

Terra Firma is a world of magic, technology, monsters, and shocking cruelty. But it's also a world of positive magic, nature, beauty, and moments where you just want to live there. Even with the crushing weight of financial oppression and savage, strange monsters waiting around the corner.

Eventually, I want to give people a game they can sink hours into and not even mind. Something that makes them think, forget, and just have a good time. Politics don't matter. All those things in real life that seemed so important fade away. Because on Terra Firma, fortune favors the truly courageous and bold—those unafraid to take the chance and reach for the gold.

If I give even a fraction of that to someone, I've done what I set out to do. If I make you a bit afraid, a bit happy, a bit overjoyed—give you that feeling of coming home—I've done my job.

Welcome to Terra Firma. Your fate is in your hands. Make your own happy ending. Or be monster poop. Your choice.
